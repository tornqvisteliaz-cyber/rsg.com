Republic RC-3 Seabee

An amphibious classic built for water, land and short coastal flights.

Built for both water and land

The Seabee is not a normal general-aviation aircraft. It is a flying boat with a pusher propeller and an all-metal hull.

First flown in the 1940s, the Republic RC-3 Seabee was designed as a four-seat amphibious aircraft for private and utility use. It could leave a lake in the morning and land at a small airfield later the same day.

Our version for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 focuses on that unique character: water handling, visual detail and a cockpit that feels mechanical rather than modern airliner-clean.
