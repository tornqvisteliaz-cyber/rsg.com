Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

RSG Software (“we”, “us”) operates this website and related customer services. Contact: support@rsgsoftware.site

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

  • Account data: name, email address, password (stored as a secure hash)

3. Why we use your data

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process orders and provide support
  • Send important service emails (welcome, verification, security)
  • Protect against abuse and unauthorized access
  • Improve our website and services

4. Legal basis (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process data based on:

  • Contract – to provide your account and purchases
  • Legitimate interest – security, fraud prevention, service improvement
  • Consent – where required (for example optional marketing)
  • Legal obligation – where we must keep records

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted processors that help us run the service, such as hosting (e.g. Render), email delivery (e.g. Resend), and payment providers — only as needed to provide the service.

6. Retention

We keep account and order data while your account is active and as long as needed for support, legal and accounting purposes. You may request account deletion; some records may remain where legally required.

7. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Data portability

Contact us at support@rsgsoftware.site to exercise these rights.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as password hashing, HTTPS, session controls and access restrictions for admin systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies/session storage required to keep you logged in and run the website securely. We do not use non-essential marketing cookies unless clearly stated later.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
