About the Seabee

The Republic RC-3 Seabee comes from the 1940s. It was built as a small flying boat for private and utility use, with a pusher propeller and a metal hull.

That is the aircraft we are making for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024: short coastal hops, lake landings and the odd shape that makes the Seabee easy to recognize.

  • Detailed exterior and interior
  • Water and land operations
  • Custom systems and sound
