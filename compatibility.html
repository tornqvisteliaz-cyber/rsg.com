Compatibility

The aircraft, controls and systems HeavyPro supports.

Aircraft profiles

HeavyPro picks a profile when it recognises the loaded aircraft. Any other aircraft uses the closest generic profile, and every profile can be tuned.

System and controls

SystemWindows 11, 64-bit. Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, Microsoft Store or Steam.
ControlsAny joystick, yoke or sidestick bound to the elevator and aileron axes. Rudder pedals are optional.
Not supportedMicrosoft Flight Simulator 2020, Xbox consoles, and other simulators such as X-Plane.
