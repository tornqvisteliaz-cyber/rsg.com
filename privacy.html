Privacy policy

What we collect, why, for how long, and your rights.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data rotaidem.com, the HeavyPro app, RotaHub and the Rotaidem Discord bot use, why, for how long, and what your rights are. We keep it short and in plain words.

Who we are
Rotaidem

We are responsible for your data (the “controller” under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). Questions or requests: write to the email address above.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers. We do not make automated decisions about you.

Who helps us (processors)

These companies handle data for us, only for the purposes above, under data processing agreements:

  • STRATO (Germany): hosts the website and database.
  • Stripe: takes card payments on its own checkout page. We never see your card number.
  • Resend: sends our emails (licence keys, confirmations, replies, newsletters).
  • Discord: only if you use our Discord server or link your account.
  • PayPal: pays commissions to affiliates.
  • Endorsely: affiliate tracking, only if you accept it in the cookie banner.
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode): a video loads only when you press play.
  • Links to Trustpilot and social media are plain links: nothing loads until you click them.

Some of these companies are based in the USA or may process data there. Transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the EU's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it (My account → Delete my account).
  • Orders and payment records: 7 years, as Swedish bookkeeping law requires. After you delete your account they no longer show your name or email.
  • IP addresses: downloads 30 days, admin logs 180 days, failed licence checks 2 days.
  • Logged-in devices: until you log out, or after 60 days without use.
  • Messages to us: deleted 2 years after they were handled.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe (every email has a one-click link).
  • Discord reminders: 30 days after they were sent; giveaway entries: 90 days after the giveaway.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, download, or delete your data, to limit how we use it, or object to it, and you can withdraw a consent at any time. Most of it you can do yourself: My account → Download my data and Delete my account; cookie choices under Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. For anything else, email us and we answer within a month.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se) or the authority in your own country.

Children

Our services are not for children under 13. If you are under 18, you need a parent's or guardian's permission to buy. If you believe a child under 13 gave us data, contact us and we delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored as secure hashes, the site only works over HTTPS, admin access is limited and logged, and licence tokens are signed.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we tell you by email or on the website before it applies. See also our cookie policy, terms and refund policy.
