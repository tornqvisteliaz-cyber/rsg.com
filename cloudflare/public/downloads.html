Trouble downloading or installing?

"Windows protected your PC"

Windows shows this for new programs that are not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer is safe: you can compare its SHA-256 checksum with the one listed here.

The browser blocks the file

Chrome or Edge may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Open the downloads list (Ctrl+J), click the file's ⋯ menu and choose Keep (Edge: Keep anyway). Or use the .zip download instead.

Antivirus or company network

Some antivirus programs and work networks remove .exe files. Download the .zip, right-click it and choose Extract all, then run HeavyProSetup.exe. Still stuck? Email Eliaz@rotaidem.com and we send you a link.
