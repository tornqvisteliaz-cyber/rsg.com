Trouble downloading or installing?

"Windows protected your PC"

Windows shows this for new programs that are not code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. RotaHub is safe to run.

The browser blocks the file

Chrome or Edge may say the file "isn't commonly downloaded". Open the downloads list (Ctrl+J), click the file's ⋯ menu and choose Keep (Edge: Keep anyway).

Antivirus or company network

Some antivirus programs and work networks remove .exe files. Allow RotaHub.exe in your antivirus, or download it at home. Still stuck? Email Eliaz@rotaidem.com and we help you.
