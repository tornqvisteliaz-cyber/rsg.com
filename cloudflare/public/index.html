A joystick is not a yoke

A short desktop stick reacts instantly. An A320 or a 777 does not. HeavyPro calculates how the real aircraft would answer your stick, from its type, weight, airspeed and flight phase, and gives your controls that heavy, realistic feel: pitch and roll build up and settle the way they do on a real airliner.

It does not touch aircraft files or flight models, and it steps aside whenever the autopilot is flying.
