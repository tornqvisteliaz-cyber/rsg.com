Introducing HeavyPro

A joystick is not a yoke

A short desktop stick reacts instantly. An A320 or a 777 does not. HeavyPro sits between your controls and the simulator and shapes every input, so pitch and roll build up and settle the way they do on a real airliner.

It does not touch aircraft files or flight models, and it steps aside whenever the autopilot is flying.

How HeavyPro works
