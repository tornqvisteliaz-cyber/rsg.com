Download RotaHub

1. Open RotaHub

Run RotaHub.exe from your downloads. It installs itself and adds a Start menu shortcut. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.

2. Log in

Use your Rotaidem account, the same as on this website. No account yet? Create one first.

3. Press Install

Pick the product in your library and press Install. RotaHub puts it in place, activates your licence key and keeps it up to date.
