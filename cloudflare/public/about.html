About Rotaidem

An independent studio making software for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

Who we are

Rotaidem is a small team of flight simulation enthusiasts. We started with a simple question: why does a desktop joystick make a 300-tonne airliner feel like a light aircraft?

HeavyPro is our answer, and our first product. We build carefully, test with real pilots and release when it is ready.

How we work

  • Fewer, better products. We would rather ship one thing that works well than many that half work.
  • Grounded in real flying. Behaviour is checked against how the real aircraft respond, and tuned with feedback from pilots.
  • We listen. Bug reports and suggestions go straight to the people who write the code.
