Installation guide

Get HeavyPro running in a few minutes.

  1. Download HeavyPro

    Log in and download the installer from Downloads or My account.

  2. Run the installer

    Follow the steps. HeavyPro installs as a normal Windows app and adds a Start menu shortcut.

  3. Bind your controls in MSFS

    In the simulator's control settings, bind your stick and pedals to Elevator Axis, Aileron Axis and Rudder Axis.

  4. Start a flight

    Open HeavyPro, then load a flight. It connects automatically, or press Connect.

  5. Move the stick once

    The status panel shows the route as Active. Choose a preset and fly.

Having trouble?

Most problems come from axes bound to a different control. Check the FAQ, or email us with a screenshot of the HeavyPro status panel.
