Frequently asked questions

Which simulator does HeavyPro work with?

Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 on Windows 11 (64-bit).

Does HeavyPro change my aircraft?

No. It does not modify aircraft files, flight models or aerodynamic forces. It only shapes the stick and rudder input it passes to the simulator.

How do I set up my controls?

In MSFS, bind your stick and pedals to Elevator Axis, Aileron Axis and Rudder Axis. Start HeavyPro, load a flight and move the stick once. The status panel will show the route as active.

What happens when the autopilot is on?

HeavyPro passes your input through unchanged whenever the autopilot is engaged, the app is switched off, or a flight is loading.

Does it work with fly-by-wire aircraft?

Yes, but Airbus aircraft such as the Fenix A320 use their own control laws, so the effect is softer than on conventional aircraft.

Where do I find my licences?

Log in and open My account. Your orders and licences are listed there.
