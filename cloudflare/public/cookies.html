Cookie policy

What this site stores in your browser, and your choices.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Cookies and similar storage (like your browser's local storage) let a website remember things. Here is everything rotaidem.com stores in your browser.

Always on (necessary)

NameWhat it doesHow long
rsg_token, rsg_name, rsg_adminKeep you logged in and show your nameUntil you log out (or 60 days unused)
rsg_consentRemembers your cookie choiceUntil you change it or clear your browser
Small settings for staff (admin pages only)Remember which admin tab was openUntil cleared

Only if you accept (affiliate tracking)

NameWhat it doesHow long
rsg_ref, rsg_ref_hitRemember the partner link you came from, so that partner gets credit if you buy30 days
Endorsely (endorsely.com)Our affiliate partner's script; it credits referred sales and stores its own identifierAs set by Endorsely (see their privacy policy)

On other services' pages

  • Stripe uses its own cookies on its checkout page to take the payment safely and prevent fraud.
  • YouTube videos use the privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play.
  • Links to Discord, Trustpilot and social media load nothing until you click them.

We use no advertising or analytics cookies. The website's server sets no cookies.

Change your choice

Press Cookie settings at the bottom of any page. Saying no to affiliate tracking changes nothing else on the site. You can also clear site data in your browser settings.
