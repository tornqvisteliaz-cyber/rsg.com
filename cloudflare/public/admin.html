      Admin page password

      An extra password that everyone must enter after logging in, before the admin page opens. So even if someone gets hold of a staff login, they cannot get in without it. Each unlock lasts 12 hours or until that person logs out. Forgotten it? Over SSH run python3 rotaidem/strato/setup.py admin-password.

      Blocklist

      Stop spam and abuse

      One per line: a full email (spammer@example.com), a whole domain (@example.com) or an IP address (203.0.113.7, or 203.0.113. for the whole range). Blocked people cannot sign up; their contact messages and newsletter sign-ups are dropped without telling them.
